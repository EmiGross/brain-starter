# Intelligence/

Knowledge generated by working: meetings, decisions, archived projects.

## Folders

- `meetings/` — meeting and call notes: `YYYY-MM-DD-{title}.md`, attendees as `[[wikilinks]]`
- `decisions/entscheidungs-log.md` — running decision log (newest on top)
- `archive/` — completed projects, moved here from `Projects/`

## Decision log entry shape

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — Kurztitel
**Entscheidung:** was entschieden wurde
**Warum:** die Begründung
**Betrifft:** [[Projekt]] / [[Person]]
```
